Contemporary
Classic Fusion

A full refurbishment and rooftop extension transformed this traditional Holland Park house into a spacious four-bedroom, three-bathroom family home. Classic architectural character is retained while clean contemporary interventions introduce light, flexibility and a stronger connection to the surrounding views.

The rooftop addition brings two bedrooms and a bathroom beneath crisp zinc detailing and generous energy-efficient windows. Inside, neutral tones, natural materials and bespoke joinery create calm, connected spaces designed for modern family life.

Our Story

Founded by
Pauline Malaquin.

Pauline Malaquin founded Studio Malaqueen with a clear conviction: that great spaces are found through an open mind and a readiness to challenge conventions. Working across architecture, interiors, branding and construction, the studio discovers opportunities in unexpected places.

We meticulously curate each project — emphasising sustainability, durability and well-being — and find the elegance that lives within simplicity. Our inspiration comes from the people we work with and the places we work within.

Questions

Things people
often ask us

What types of projects do you undertake?
+
We work across architecture, interior design, refurbishment, branding and construction. Each project is a thoughtful response to its unique surroundings, harmonising with both the built and natural environments. From single rooms to whole buildings, we manage every detail from concept to completion.
Do you navigate planning constraints?
+
Yes. Each project is a thoughtful response to its unique surroundings, and that includes navigating any planning constraints carefully. We work with the built and natural environment rather than against it, guiding you through the planning process as part of our full service.
At what stage should we contact you?
+
The earlier, the better. The most rewarding projects begin with open questions rather than fixed ideas. If you are thinking about a property, wondering what might be possible or simply want to explore, a conversation with us costs nothing and often reveals possibilities you had not yet considered.
How are fees structured?
+
Fees vary depending on the scope and nature of each project. We discuss this openly at the outset and provide a clear proposal before any work begins. Please get in touch to start the conversation — we are always happy to talk through what a project might involve.
What should we include in our first message?
+
A few words about your property and what you are hoping to create is more than enough to begin. You do not need drawings, a fixed brief or a confirmed budget — just a sense of the space and the life you imagine within it. We will take it from there.
